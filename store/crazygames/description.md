# One Touch — CrazyGames submission

**Title:** One Touch
**Category:** Puzzle (tags: puzzle, physics, neon, aim, logic, casual)
**Orientation:** portrait game, works in landscape on desktop (controls move to the side)
**Build:** `python3 tools/build_crazygames.py one-touch` → `dist/one-touch-crazygames.zip` (upload the zip)

## Description
One shot. Every dot. Aim the beam so that it bounces off the walls and passes through all the dots in a single shot.

One Touch is a neon puzzle about angles. Find the one line that ricochets through the whole level — then try to do it on the first attempt.

- 60 levels in four zones: Neon, Ocean, Sunset and Space
- Portals that teleport the beam and glass that breaks after one hit
- Daily Level with a streak and bonus coins
- Bonus rounds: three perfect clears in a row open a field full of coins — grab as many as you can in three shots
- Shop with skins for the cannon, the beam and the dots

## Controls
- Mouse / touch: drag on the field to aim, press Fire to shoot
- Keyboard: left / right arrows to fine-tune the angle, Space or Enter to fire
